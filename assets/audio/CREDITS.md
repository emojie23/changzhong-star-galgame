Files are kept locally so the game remains playable offline. The player crossfades tracks over approximately 1.7 seconds at chapter changes.
For faster mobile playback, the distributed copies are normalized to approximately -16 LUFS and encoded as 80 kbps MP3. The original CC0 licensing remains unchanged.
